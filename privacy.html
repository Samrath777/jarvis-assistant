Privacy Policy

In short: JARVIS runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own, and its developer never receives your data. The accounts you connect are used only to do what you ask, and your sign-ins are stored only on your computer. Nothing is sold, used for ads, or used to train AI.

Who we are

JARVIS is a personal desktop assistant made by Samrath Rawat and shared with friends. Contact: samrath.rawatt@gmail.com.

What JARVIS accesses, and only when you connect it

  • Gmail: to list, search and read your messages, save drafts and send email. Sending always waits for you to confirm on screen.
  • YouTube: to show your channel and video statistics, edit your videos' titles and descriptions, and upload a video you choose after you confirm.
  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional account: to show your posts, comments and insights, and to publish a post you approve.
  • WhatsApp (Windows): JARVIS operates the WhatsApp app on your PC to read unread chats, send a message you confirm, or start a call. It does not copy or store your chats.
  • Your computer: the microphone while you talk to JARVIS, the screen when you ask it to look or act, and system error logs to warn you about crashes.

Where your data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are saved in a settings file on your own computer, and are never sent to the developer.
  • Your requests, and the information needed to answer them, go to the AI model you set up (by default Google's Gemini API, with your own key). That is covered by that provider's terms.
  • Data from Google, Meta and other services goes directly between your computer and that service.
  • JARVIS does not sell data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Google user data

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail and YouTube data is used only to provide the features you ask for. It is never transferred to anyone else, except to the AI model you configured when your request needs it, as described above. It is never used for advertising, and no human reads it.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) data

Data from Meta's APIs is used only to show you your own Page and Instagram content and to publish what you approve, in line with Meta's Platform Terms. It is not stored beyond your computer's settings file, not shared, and not used to build profiles.

Phone remote

If you use the Android remote away from home, the connection between your phone and your PC passes through Cloudflare's tunnel service in encrypted form (HTTPS with an extra AES-256 layer). Cloudflare relays it but does not store your commands. On home Wi-Fi the phone talks to your PC directly.

Installer request form

If you request JARVIS on this website, the form sends your name, email, the answers you give and the time to Samrath through Formspree, a form service. They're used only to reply to your request and send the installer, never shared or used for marketing. Ask at samrath.rawatt@gmail.com to have them deleted.

Keeping and removing your data

JARVIS keeps sign-ins until you disconnect. You can remove access at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings › Business integrations
  • Or delete JARVIS's settings file on your computer (config/api_keys.json), or uninstall JARVIS.

Children

JARVIS is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: samrath.rawatt@gmail.com.
