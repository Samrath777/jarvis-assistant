Privacy Policy

JARVIS Assistant · Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: JARVIS runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own, and it does not sell, share or collect your data. The accounts you connect are used only to do what you ask, and your sign-ins are stored only on your computer.

Who we are

JARVIS is a personal desktop assistant made by Samrath Rawat and shared with friends. Contact: samrath.rawatt@gmail.com.

What JARVIS accesses, and only when you connect it

  • Gmail: to list, search and read your messages, save drafts and send email. Sending always waits for you to confirm on screen.
  • YouTube: to show your channel and video statistics, edit your videos' titles and descriptions, and upload a video you choose (after you confirm).
  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional account: to show your posts, comments and insights, and to publish a post you approve.
  • Your computer: the microphone while you talk to JARVIS, the screen when you ask it to do something on screen, and error logs to warn you about crashes.

Where your data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are saved in a settings file on your own computer, and never sent to us.
  • Your requests, and the information needed to answer them, are sent to the AI model you set up (by default Google's Gemini API, with your own API key), so it can reply. That is covered by Google's terms for the Gemini API.
  • Data from Google, Meta and other services goes directly between your computer and that service. JARVIS's developer never receives it.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Google user data

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail and YouTube data is used only to provide the features you ask for, and is never transferred to anyone else, except to the AI model you configured when your request needs it, as described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

JARVIS keeps sign-ins until you disconnect. You can remove access at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings > Business integrations
  • Or delete JARVIS's settings file on your computer (config/api_keys.json).

Children

JARVIS is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
