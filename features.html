Features

Everything, by voice.

Built-in skills for your computer, your accounts and your phone, plus guard rails so nothing important happens without you.

New in Space Edition

Focus timer on the island

"Start a 25-minute focus timer." A live countdown sits in the island over any app, and JARVIS tells you when it's done.

Daily digest

"What did I miss?" Unread email and who it's from, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and PC problems, in one summary.

Clipboard assistant

Copy any text, then "summarize it", "translate it", "fix it" or "reply to it". The result is ready to paste.

Voice

A conversation, not commands.

JARVIS listens and replies in real time with a natural voice. Interrupt it mid-sentence, change your mind, or talk in your own language.

  • Wake word, push-to-talk, or always listening
  • Morning brief with your news, weather and schedule
  • Remembers what matters to you between sessions
  • Sees your screen or camera when you ask it to look
YOU
The island

Live subtitles, always on top.

A sleek black pill, like a phone's dynamic island, shows what you and JARVIS say, timed to the voice, over any app or game.

  • Grows for subtitles, shrinks back when quiet
  • Hover for mic, stop and open controls
  • Right-click: text size, width, lines, opacity, who to show
  • Sits under the menu bar on Mac

Autopilot

"Do this for me."

JARVIS looks at your screen, decides the next step, clicks and types, and checks that it worked. Then it repeats until the job is done.

  • Shopping: compares options, adds the best to your cart
  • Games: plays turn-based games like Pokémon for you
  • Forms, settings, playlists, anything with buttons
  • Hard stop before pay, checkout, sign-in or passwords

Social

Your accounts, one drawer.

Sign in with a normal Google or Facebook popup. Then ask JARVIS to check, reply, post or call.

  • Gmail: unread, search, read, draft, send (with confirm)
  • YouTube: channel stats, uploads, edit titles, upload videos
  • Instagram and Facebook Page: posts, comments, insights, publish
  • WhatsApp (Windows): unread chats, messages, voice and video calls

PC health

It notices before you do.

JARVIS watches for the problems that matter, skips the noise, and tells you on screen, on your phone, and out loud when it's serious.

  • App crashes and hangs, blue screens, kernel panics
  • Disk errors, driver resets, hardware faults
  • Drive almost full, RAM or CPU maxed out, overheating
  • "Any problems with my PC?" gives you a full report

Phone remote

Call your PC from anywhere.

Open the JARVIS app on Android and talk to your computer over a private tunnel, from home Wi-Fi or across the world.

  • Two-way voice call with JARVIS
  • Type commands, send files to your PC
  • PC alerts as phone notifications
  • Pairing code and certificate pinning keep strangers out
