Security

Powerful.
On a short leash.

An assistant that can click, type and post for you needs rules it can't talk its way around. These are JARVIS's.

No JARVIS servers

There is no JARVIS cloud. The app runs on your computer and talks directly to the services you connect: Google, Meta, and the AI model you choose.

Sign-ins stay local

Google and Facebook tokens are saved only in a settings file on your PC. Revoke them any time from your Google or Facebook account.

A confirm button the AI can't press

Sending email, posting, uploading and WhatsApp messages show a CONFIRM button on your screen. Only a real click runs them; the AI can't fake it.

Autopilot hard stops

Autopilot refuses to click buy, pay, checkout, subscribe or delete-account buttons, and never types into password fields. Those are always yours.

Screen text isn't an order

Emails, web pages and messages JARVIS reads are marked as untrusted. A page that says "ignore your instructions" gets reported to you, not obeyed.

An encrypted phone link

The phone remote uses HTTPS plus AES-256 inside it, a one-time 8-character pairing code, limits on wrong guesses, expiring sessions and a pinned certificate.

Stop button

Always in your hands

How do I stop JARVIS mid-task?

Say "stop", press Esc, click stop on the island, or for Autopilot, throw the mouse into a screen corner.

How do I disconnect an account?

Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Facebook and Instagram: Settings › Business integrations. Or delete config/api_keys.json.
