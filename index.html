FAQ

Questions

Is JARVIS free?

Yes. JARVIS itself is free. It talks through Google's Gemini API using your own key, which has a free tier.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, on macOS 11 or newer, on both Apple silicon and Intel. Everything works except WhatsApp control, which needs the Windows WhatsApp app.

Can it spend my money or post without asking?

No. Sending email, posting, uploading and WhatsApp messages wait for you to press Confirm, and Autopilot always stops before checkout or payment.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your computer. Your requests go to the AI model you choose, and your account data goes straight between your PC and Google or Meta. See the privacy policy.

Why does Google say the app is unverified?

JARVIS is a personal project, not a company app that has paid for Google's review. Click Advanced, then Go to JARVIS, once. It's still Google's own sign-in page.
