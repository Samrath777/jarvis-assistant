PERSONAL AI ASSISTANT

JARVIS

JARVIS is a voice assistant that runs on your own Windows or Mac computer. You talk to it, and it helps with your PC, your email and your social accounts. It's a personal project, shared with friends.

What it can do

  • Answer questions and control your computer by voice
  • Read and draft Gmail messages (sending always asks you first)
  • Show your YouTube channel stats and upload videos you choose
  • Show and publish to your Facebook Page and Instagram professional account
  • Warn you about crashes and errors on your computer

Your data stays with you

JARVIS has no servers of its own. Your sign-ins are saved only on your own computer, and you can disconnect any account at any time. Read the privacy policy.
